Multiple anatomical concepts may refer to the same element. Specifically FJ1843 is mapped to tentorium cerebelli, region of dura mater, and subdivision of cranial dura mater. These are not three separate meshes and do not establish a complete cranial dura shell. Subarachnoid-space/incisure matches are contextual brain surfaces, not an arachnoid membrane. The 99%-reduction source is for feasibility inspection; fine vascular fidelity requires comparison with higher-detail source geometry.
