Privacy notice

Updated 8 October 2026.

MAPPED is a neuroscience research and learning web app operated by Nemanja Arsenijevic. For privacy questions or requests, contact mapped.nac@gmail.com.

Optional product analytics

With your permission, we use PostHog’s EU Cloud project to understand feature use and improve MAPPED. We collect screen views, selected anatomical regions and atlas, and events indicating project creation, source saving, new note saving and signup submission. Signup submission does not mean email verification is complete.

PostHog also receives standard browser, device and session metadata. A random browser identifier is stored in localStorage to measure return visits on the same browser. It is not linked to your account by this integration. Such identifiers are pseudonymous, not a guarantee of anonymity. Network requests necessarily expose your IP address to the receiving service.

We do not send private note text, project names or descriptions, paper content, email addresses, passwords or account IDs through this analytics integration. Session recording, automatic click capture and surveys are disabled; URL and query-string event properties are excluded.

Our basis for optional analytics is your consent. No PostHog collection starts until you accept. Rejecting does not restrict access to MAPPED. Use Analytics preferences in MAPPED at any time to change your choice. Withdrawal stops future collection and clears PostHog’s local identifier, but does not automatically delete previously collected events. Your accept/reject choice is stored separately so we can respect it; browser storage remains until cleared or changed.

PostHog’s published event-retention window is one year on its Free plan and seven years on paid plans. The window limits which events can be queried; it is not an automatic deletion guarantee. The applicable MAPPED subscription has not yet been confirmed in this notice. Contact us for the current plan or to request deletion. See PostHog’s retention rules and privacy policy. EU project hosting does not mean every provider administrative process or subprocessor is located exclusively in the EU.

Hosting and basic website statistics

Cloudflare serves MAPPED and may process technical request data to deliver and protect the site. Cloudflare Web Analytics provides aggregate traffic and performance statistics. Its analytics beacon does not use cookies or localStorage for usage metrics. This collection is separate from optional PostHog analytics. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and Web Analytics information.

Accounts, research workspaces and community content

Supabase provides authentication and database services. Account flows process email addresses and authentication data to provide the service you request. MAPPED stores workspace data and settings in your browser, including saved sources, notes and projects. Some account and community features also use Supabase. Content you explicitly publish to community features may be visible to others. Avoid putting sensitive personal or patient information in notes or public contributions.

Account and workspace storage needed to provide requested features is separate from optional analytics. Data remains in browser storage until removed or cleared; database content follows the relevant account/content lifecycle and provider backup retention. Contact us for account or database deletion requests. See Supabase’s privacy policy.

Contact and external services

The optional feedback survey opens a Google Form when requested. Google receives requests when the form loads and processes the answers you submit. See Google’s privacy policy.

The contact form prepares a draft in your email app; you decide whether to send it. If you send a message, we process its contents to respond. External research links and services have their own privacy practices.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, object to relevant processing, and withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. You may complain to your local data protection authority. Email mapped.nac@gmail.com to make a request. Because analytics is not linked to accounts, identifying particular events may require your browser identifier; do not send passwords or authentication tokens.
