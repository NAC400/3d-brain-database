MAPPED selected the falx cerebri, left/right tentorium and sixteen named dural venous sinus objects from NervousSystem100.fbx and CardioVascular41.fbx. It preserved world transforms, excluded helper labels and unrelated meshes, triangulated source polygons via Three.js FBXLoader, regenerated normals and applied one shared experimental similarity fit to SPL/NAC structure-centre proxies. No outer dura, arachnoid or pia membrane was fabricated. No new mirrored meshes were generated. Source files contain symmetric bilateral anatomy. These adapted Z-Anatomy model assets are released under CC BY-SA 4.0; preserve attribution and the ShareAlike terms when distributing adaptations. Source third-party notices are preserved without applying the current BodyParts3D CC BY 4.0 licence retroactively to this older derivative collection.

The unrelated inner-ear and kidney assets carrying NonCommercial notices were not selected. Brain-reference objects were used only for fitting proxies; their mesh geometry is not distributed in this layer.

Source file SHA256 hashes, actual object coverage, transformation and validation limits are recorded in coverage-and-alignment.json. Retrieved 5 October 2026. No source institution endorses MAPPED. For illustrative research and learning; not anatomically validated and not for clinical decisions.
