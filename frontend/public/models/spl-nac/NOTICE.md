This is a modified distribution, not the original atlas: brain surfaces were selected, converted from VTK to GLB, rotated into Y-up coordinates, and given recomputed normals. No hemisphere was generated by reflection. The MRI/label volumes, external head structures, and sulcal line models are not included. No endorsement by the source institutions is implied.

This is a single-subject reference atlas, not patient-specific anatomy or a clinically validated MAPPED model. Review status and conversion provenance are in manifest.json.
